TRANSPORTATION TO CAMPUS
The Pittsburgh International 
Airport (PIT) is 22 miles from 
the Carnegie Mellon University 
campus. Travel time to campus 
is 45 minutes. Car rentals are 
available at the airport.
UBER, LYFT OR TAXI:  Average 
taxi fare: $45.00. 
HOTEL SHUTTLES:  (Highly 
encouraged): Often offered  
by hotels within a few miles  
of campus.
BUS:  28X Airport Flyer Bus (Port 
Authority Transit), stops at the 
Carnegie Mellon campus. Cost 
is $2.75 each way (exact change 
required) but free to students.
PARKING ON CAMPUS
